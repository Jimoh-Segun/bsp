Advanced Leadership -Situational leadership, Tribal Leadership

There is no single best leadership style. The best leaders adjust their style to fit the person and the task in front of them.

The leader looks at two things:

  • Competence: does this person have the skill and knowledge to do this task?
  • Commitment: do they have the confidence and motivation to do it?

The four styles

  • Directing: the leader gives clear, specific instructions and watches closely. It works best for a new person or a new task.
  • Coaching: the leader still directs but also explains the why and invites input. It works best when someone is learning but getting discouraged.
  • Supporting: the leader encourages, listens, and lets the person make more decisions. It works best for a skilled person whose confidence wavers.
  • Delegating: the leader hands over responsibility and steps back. It works best for a skilled, confident, motivated person.

Good leadership means the supervisor reads the situation and flexes, rather than treating everyone the same.
