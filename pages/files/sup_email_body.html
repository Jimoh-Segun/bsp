Supervisor Training Access: What We Need From You

To give each of your supervisors their own access to the daily training videos, we need a simple list of the supervisors who will use them. This takes about 10 minutes.

What to send us

One row per supervisor, with these three items:

Supervisor NameEmail AddressCompany Name
Jane Samplejane.sample@yourcompany.comYour Company

The shaded row is an example only.

How to send it

  • Use our Excel template (attached) and fill in one row per supervisor.
Download the Excel template
  • If you already have the supervisor information on another template, just send that
  • Email the completed file to us at support@BestPerformanceSystems.com.
  • Please send it within 1 week after you receive the program.
  • We will upload the names into our system and start sending emails onboarding and training your supervisors right away
  • Each supervisor needs their own email address. Their email is their login, and it is how we track their progress.
  • Please do not use shared or group emails (for example, safety@ or supervisors@). Two people on one login would blend their records.
  • Double-check the spelling of each email address. One wrong letter means that supervisor will not receive their access.
  • A supervisor without a work email may use a personal email.

What happens next

  • We set up each supervisor and they receive a welcome email from us.
  • Each supervisor creates their own password and signs in.
  • Supervisors can then choose any of the training videos to show their teams.
  • We will create a schedule for the supervisor to use to show the videos, and we will train the supervisors and workers to choose together the training that they want to look at next. The more they get to choose for themselves, the more they will buy into the program
  • We can report to you which videos each supervisor has completed and when they last logged in.The goal is they show at least 3 videos per week.

Questions?

Reply to the email we sent you or contact us at support@BestPerformanceSystems.com. We are glad to help.
